Conversation canvas: three options

Issue #82. One invented conversation drawn four ways: as the canvas looks today, then three options. Use the switches to compare them in both themes, at font scale 1.0 and 2.0, and at different zoom levels. These are HTML mockups using the app's real colours, close to but not exactly what Avalonia draws.

Today

baseline

What the screenshot review found: 10 px text, a fixed 200 px card with a 70 px clip on top of an 80-character cut, large ports in dark side columns, connections drawn over the cards, a mid-grey canvas in both themes, the minimap over the content, and auto-layout spacing (220 × 110 assumed) that doesn't match the real cards. Switch to 2.0 to see lines lose their ending with no "…".

Option A: fix the rules, keep the look

smallest change

Same compact cards and text sizes. Everything that is a bug or a mismatch gets fixed: one preview rule, a card that widens with the font scale, layout gaps measured from the real card, and the chrome problems (edges behind cards, slimmer ports, a theme-matched canvas, the minimap kept clear).

    Option B: readable cards

    recommended

    Everything in A, plus text a writer can read at 100% without opening the detail panel: 12 px body text, wider cards, and cards that grow to fit their line up to four lines. Layout places each card by its real height.

      Option C: readable cards with zoom levels

      most work

      Option B, plus a level of detail that follows the zoom: zoomed out, cards collapse to their header and a single line so a big conversation's shape is readable; zoomed in, cards show up to eight lines. Try the zoom switch.

        Why I recommend B

        It answers the review's main question.At 100% a writer can read a typical line on the card, which is most of what the canvas is for. A fixes the bugs but keeps text that's hard to read on a 1080p screen.
        It's the honest fix for font scale.Cards that widen and grow with the text mean the accessibility setting shows more, never less, which is #82's done-when.
        C can come later.Zoom levels only help on very large conversations, and they need B's growing cards first. B leaves the door open without the cost now.
        What B costs.Bigger cards mean big conversations take more room: about 20% wider and somewhat taller than today. Fit-to-screen shrinks them further on a hub with 100+ nodes.

        Not part of these options

        The review saw every speaker's header in the same olive-brown. The tokens already give NPC, player, narrator, script and bark headers different colours (as drawn here), so that looks like a bug in how the canvas picks the colour, not a design choice. I'd file it separately. The same goes for finding 9 (menus and panel titles ignore font scale), which belongs to the app chrome, not the canvas.
